A Chemical Laboratory That Can Learn as It Works

We integrate robotic synthesis, automated characterization and AI-guided experiment planning into a traceable closed loop. Each result updates the model, helping the platform choose experiments that are both informative and relevant to the scientific objective.

AutomationRoboticsOrchestration SystemAI

Reliable Automation

Modular hardware and software coordinate synthesis, sampling and measurement while preserving experimental context and reproducibility.

Adaptive Decisions

Bayesian optimization and active-learning strategies balance exploration with performance to select high-value experiments.

Learning from Every Outcome

Successful, unsuccessful and uncertain results are captured in a common data structure and returned to the model for continual refinement.
